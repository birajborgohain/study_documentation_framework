CAT Examination Syllabus
========================

The **Common Admission Test (CAT)** is a national-level entrance examination
conducted for admission to postgraduate management programs, including the
Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) and several other leading business
schools in India.

Unlike many competitive examinations, **CAT does not prescribe an official
fixed syllabus**. However, based on previous years' examinations, the syllabus
can be broadly divided into the following three sections.

Verbal Ability and Reading Comprehension (VARC)
-----------------------------------------------

This section evaluates a candidate's reading ability, vocabulary, grammar, and
verbal reasoning skills.

Topics
~~~~~~

* Reading Comprehension
* Para Jumbles
* Para Summary
* Odd Sentence Out
* Sentence Completion
* Vocabulary
* Synonyms and Antonyms
* Idioms and Phrases
* Grammar
* Fill in the Blanks
* Critical Reasoning
* Verbal Reasoning

Data Interpretation and Logical Reasoning (DILR)
------------------------------------------------

This section tests analytical thinking, logical reasoning, and the ability to
interpret numerical and graphical information.

Data Interpretation
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Tables
* Bar Graphs
* Line Graphs
* Pie Charts
* Caselets
* Mixed Graphs
* Radar Charts
* Data Sufficiency
* Missing Data Interpretation

Logical Reasoning
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Seating Arrangement
* Linear Arrangement
* Circular Arrangement
* Scheduling
* Puzzles
* Blood Relations
* Family Tree
* Direction Sense
* Ranking
* Order and Ranking
* Binary Logic
* Venn Diagrams
* Games and Tournaments
* Network Problems
* Routes and Maps
* Cubes
* Clocks and Calendars
* Logical Sequences

Quantitative Aptitude (QA)
--------------------------

This section assesses mathematical concepts, problem-solving ability, and
numerical aptitude.

Arithmetic
~~~~~~~~~~

* Percentages
* Profit and Loss
* Simple Interest
* Compound Interest
* Ratio and Proportion
* Partnership
* Average
* Mixture and Alligation
* Time and Work
* Pipes and Cisterns
* Time, Speed, and Distance
* Boats and Streams
* Relative Speed

Algebra
~~~~~~~

* Linear Equations
* Quadratic Equations
* Polynomials
* Inequalities
* Logarithms
* Functions
* Progressions (AP, GP, HP)
* Surds and Indices

Number System
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Divisibility Rules
* Factors and Multiples
* Prime Numbers
* Remainders
* HCF and LCM
* Cyclicity
* Base System

Geometry
~~~~~~~~

* Lines and Angles
* Triangles
* Quadrilaterals
* Circles
* Polygons
* Coordinate Geometry
* Solid Geometry

Mensuration
~~~~~~~~~~~

* Area
* Perimeter
* Surface Area
* Volume

Modern Mathematics
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Permutation and Combination
* Probability
* Set Theory
* Venn Diagrams

Exam Pattern
------------

+--------------------------------+-------------------------+
| Section                        | Major Topics            |
+================================+=========================+
| Verbal Ability & Reading       | RC, Grammar, Vocabulary |
| Comprehension (VARC)           | Verbal Reasoning        |
+--------------------------------+-------------------------+
| Data Interpretation & Logical  | DI Sets, Graphs,        |
| Reasoning (DILR)               | Puzzles, Arrangements   |
+--------------------------------+-------------------------+
| Quantitative Aptitude (QA)     | Arithmetic, Algebra,    |
|                                | Geometry, Number System |
+--------------------------------+-------------------------+

Preparation Strategy
--------------------

* Build strong conceptual understanding before learning shortcuts.
* Practice a variety of problems from each topic.
* Solve previous years' CAT questions regularly.
* Take sectional and full-length mock tests.
* Analyze mistakes after every mock test.
* Revise formulas and important concepts frequently.
* Focus on improving both speed and accuracy.
