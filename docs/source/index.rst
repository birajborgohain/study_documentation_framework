Competitive Examination Preparation Portal
==========================================

Welcome to the **Competitive Examination Preparation Portal**, a structured
study resource designed for the preparation of major competitive examinations
such as **CAT**, **RBI Grade B**, **RBI Assistant**, **SBI PO**, **SBI Clerk**,
and other banking examinations.

This website organizes the complete syllabus into well-structured topics,
making it easy to study, revise, and track progress. Each subject is divided
into individual concepts with theory, solved examples, practice problems,
previous year questions, and revision notes.

The primary objective of this portal is to transform scattered notes into a
searchable, well-organized knowledge base that can be continuously expanded
throughout the preparation journey. Built using **VS Code** and **Sphinx**, it
also serves as an introduction to modern technical documentation practices and
can later be published using **GitHub** and **Read the Docs**.

Whether preparing for aptitude tests, logical reasoning, English, banking
awareness, or interview rounds, this portal provides a single place to
organize, document, and master every aspect of the examination syllabus.
