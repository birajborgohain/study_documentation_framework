Linear Equations in One Variable
================================

Overview
--------

A **linear equation in one variable** is an equation in which the highest power
of the variable is **1**. These equations are among the most fundamental topics
in algebra and frequently appear in competitive examinations such as **CAT**,
**RBI Grade B**, **SBI PO**, **IBPS PO**, and other aptitude tests.

The objective is to determine the value of the unknown variable that satisfies
the equation.

Concept
-------

A linear equation in one variable has the general form

.. math::

   ax+b=0

where

* :math:`a` and :math:`b` are constants.
* :math:`a \neq 0`
* :math:`x` is the unknown variable.

The solution is obtained by isolating the variable.

Formula
-------

Starting from

.. math::

   ax+b=0

Subtract :math:`b` from both sides.

.. math::

   ax=-b

Divide both sides by :math:`a`.

.. math::

   x=-\frac{b}{a}

Example 1
---------

Solve

.. math::

   5x+10=0

**Step 1**

Subtract 10 from both sides.

.. math::

   5x=-10

**Step 2**

Divide by 5.

.. math::

   x=-2

Therefore,

.. math::

   \boxed{x=-2}

Example 2
---------

Solve

.. math::

   4x-7=13

Move the constant term.

.. math::

   4x=13+7

.. math::

   4x=20

Divide by 4.

.. math::

   x=5

Answer

.. math::

   \boxed{x=5}

Example 3
---------

Solve

.. math::

   \frac{x}{3}+4=9

Subtract 4 from both sides.

.. math::

   \frac{x}{3}=5

Multiply both sides by 3.

.. math::

   x=15

Shortcut
--------

For equations of the form

.. math::

   ax+b=c

directly use

.. math::

   x=\frac{c-b}{a}

Common Mistakes
---------------

* Forgetting to change the sign when moving a term across the equal sign.
* Dividing by the wrong coefficient.
* Arithmetic mistakes after simplifying.

Previous Year Question (CAT Style)
----------------------------------

A number is increased by 12 and the result is three times the original number.
Find the number.

**Solution**

Let the number be :math:`x`.

According to the question,

.. math::

   x+12=3x

Rearranging,

.. math::

   12=2x

Hence,

.. math::

   x=6

Summary
-------

Key points to remember:

* Degree of a linear equation is **1**.
* General form: :math:`ax+b=0`.
* Solution:

  .. math::

     x=-\frac{b}{a}

* Always perform the same operation on both sides of the equation.
* Practice simplifying equations carefully to avoid calculation errors.
