Theory and Concepts
===================

A strong understanding of theory and concepts forms the foundation of success
in competitive examinations such as **CAT**, **RBI Grade B**, **SBI PO**, and
other banking examinations. While shortcuts and tricks can improve speed, they
are most effective when supported by a clear understanding of the underlying
mathematical principles.

The **Theory and Concepts** section introduces each topic from first
principles. Every concept is explained using simple language, step-by-step
reasoning, and illustrative examples before moving on to more advanced
applications.

Each topic typically includes:

* An overview of the concept.
* Definitions of important terms.
* Fundamental principles and formulas.
* Step-by-step worked examples.
* Visual illustrations or diagrams where appropriate.
* Common mistakes and misconceptions.
* Practical applications.
* Summary of key points.

Students are encouraged to read the theory carefully before attempting the
practice problems. Understanding *why* a method works is far more valuable than
simply memorizing formulas or solution techniques.

By building a strong conceptual foundation, students will be better equipped to
solve unfamiliar problems, recognize patterns quickly, and apply the most
appropriate solution methods during the examination.
