Introduction
============

Welcome to the **Competitive Examination Preparation Portal**, a comprehensive
learning platform designed to support preparation for competitive examinations
such as **CAT**, **RBI Grade B**, **RBI Assistant**, **SBI PO**, **SBI Clerk**,
**IBPS PO**, and other banking and management entrance examinations.

The primary goal of this website is to organize the complete examination
syllabus into a structured, searchable, and continuously expandable knowledge
base. Instead of relying on scattered notebooks, PDFs, and bookmarks, all study
materials are collected in one place and organized topic by topic for efficient
learning and revision.

This documentation is developed using **Visual Studio Code (VS Code)** and
**Sphinx**, allowing the content to be viewed as a professional documentation
website. As the material grows, it can be version-controlled with **Git**,
hosted on **GitHub**, and published online using **Read the Docs**, ensuring
that the latest content is always available.

Overview
========

The website is organized into several major sections, each focusing on a
specific aspect of competitive examination preparation.

Syllabus
---------

The **Syllabus** section provides a detailed breakdown of the topics covered in
each examination, helping students understand the scope of the syllabus before
beginning their preparation.

Theory and Concepts
-------------------

The **Theory and Concepts** section introduces the fundamental ideas behind each
topic. Concepts are explained using clear definitions, formulas, diagrams,
worked examples, and intuitive reasoning to build a strong conceptual
foundation.

Practice Problems
-----------------

The **Practice Problems** section contains carefully selected questions arranged
by difficulty level—from basic exercises to advanced examination-style
problems—to develop both accuracy and speed.

Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
------------------------------

The **Previous Year Questions (PYQs)** section familiarizes students with the
pattern and difficulty level of actual examinations while reinforcing important
concepts through real questions.

Additional Resources
--------------------

Additional sections include:

* Formula Sheets
* Revision Notes
* Mock Tests
* Current Affairs
* Vocabulary
* Logical Reasoning
* Data Interpretation
* Study Planners

These resources help students monitor their progress and prepare
systematically for the examination.

Objectives
==========

The objectives of this portal are to:

* Organize the complete syllabus in a logical and structured manner.
* Build strong conceptual understanding before introducing shortcuts and
  problem-solving techniques.
* Provide extensive practice through graded problem sets.
* Develop speed, accuracy, and analytical thinking.
* Maintain concise revision notes and formula sheets.
* Track learning progress using study plans and mock tests.
* Create a reusable knowledge base that can be updated throughout the
  preparation journey.

By following this structured approach, students can study more efficiently,
identify their strengths and weaknesses, and build the confidence required to
perform well in competitive examinations.
