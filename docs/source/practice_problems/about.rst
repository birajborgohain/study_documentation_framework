Practice Problems
=================

Practice problems are an essential part of competitive examination preparation.
Simply reading theory is not enough to develop the speed, accuracy, and
problem-solving skills required for examinations such as **CAT**, **RBI Grade B**,
**SBI PO**, and other banking examinations.

Each practice problem provides an opportunity to apply the concepts learned in
the theory section. By solving a variety of questions, students become familiar
with different problem types, recognize common patterns, and learn efficient
solution techniques.

The practice problems in this section are organized by **difficulty level**:

* **Easy** – Reinforces fundamental concepts and basic calculations.
* **Medium** – Develops analytical thinking and problem-solving strategies.
* **Hard** – Challenges conceptual understanding and prepares students for
  high-level competitive examination questions.

To maximize learning, follow these guidelines:

1. Attempt each problem without looking at the solution.
2. Record the time taken to solve each question.
3. Compare your solution with the recommended approach.
4. Analyze any mistakes and understand why they occurred.
5. Revisit difficult problems after a few days to reinforce learning.
6. Maintain a notebook of important formulas, shortcuts, and common errors.

Regular practice not only improves conceptual understanding but also increases
speed and confidence. Consistent problem-solving is one of the most effective
ways to prepare for competitive examinations.
